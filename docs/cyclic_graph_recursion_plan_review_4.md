# Fourth adversarial review of `cyclic_graph_recursion_plan.md` (version 4) and `vascnet/fluxnet.py`

Scope: the plan's handling table checked against its own text and the code, and new attacks on the version-4 decisions. Every number was run (scripts in the session's scratchpad, not committed); the 30 prototype tests pass in 1.4 s. Line numbers are from `notebooks/development copy 2.py` unless a file is named. Findings marked **[table]** attack a handling the plan's table claims.

## Verdict: would each section, as written, improve automatic annotation?

**35 (generator).** Yes, as a precondition: nothing is scored until loops, a plexus, gaps, per-junction `n` and a velocity profile exist. Nothing breaks it. Settled by: old seeds bit-identical, the per-seed counts.

**36 (junction cells).** In theory yes for thick junctions; unproven for capillaries, where everything hangs on check 1 (the 4 px ring on the *oriented* score, not the density). Settled by check 1 and the recall column after section 35 item 11.

**37 (cyclic graph).** Yes in theory, and it is the cheapest section. Two things would mislead: the "`extend_ends` only from ends one field length long" restriction is vacuous (finding 8), and the sixth confidence term comes from section 38, which the order of work puts last, so the first principle's number is measured on five terms and the plan should say so. Settled by `told_apart` ≥ 0.75 on five terms, on the real burst.

**38 (renderer and residual).** Partly. The core matched filter is sound in form but is the existing `overlap` test (5845–5848) with more pixels, and the plan does not say what z it reaches at a capillary core against `Z_PASS` = 3; until it does, four-arm typing stays geometry's (finding 7). The held-out whitened RMS is read on quiet pixels, where the drawing draws nothing: it scores the noise estimator, not the model (finding 6). Settled by: the core z on synthetic crossings against recorded one-lumen junctions, per core radius, and a whitened RMS over the pixels the drawing touches.

**39 (recursion).** Not as written. "No loop in step 6" is true within a round, but step 6 runs every round, section 27 re-reads with the flux test among its tests at step 2 of the next round, and `ROUNDS_MOST` = 5: that is the readings → solve → readings loop the third review showed oscillating, with the cap raised from 3 to 5 (finding 3). The moves themselves (removal, merge, split, J as a veto) are sound in theory. Settled by: readings changed per round on a plexus burst, reaching 0 before the cap.

**40 (flux network).** On trees with measured arms and the *default* lumen confidences: yes (my trees: +0 to +4 points at 0.45, +4 to +12 at 0.15, flips 18:5 over four seeds at 0.45). With section 27's confidences passed as `lumen_confidence`, as the text says they are: no. The set-aside then costs less than one flip at nearly every lumen, 9–28 of 127 lumens are set aside, and the gain falls to 0–1 point (finding 1). On a plexus with whole-vessel errors and crossing readings, right vessels are turned round (finding 2). The bridge prior's unit is wrong at both ends of the flux range (finding 5). Settled by: the network run on the real burst's `whole_graphs` with section 27's confidences, counting set-asides against flips; a plexus test with whole-vessel errors and crossing readings.

**41 (confidences).** Yes in theory. Two-arm nodes and `extend_ends` joins have no reading and no recorded provenance, so the confidence the set-aside needs there does not exist yet (finding 13).

**42 (harness).** The real-burst row "inferred directions against `propagate_directions`" compares two inferences; it is agreement, not a score, and the text should say so. Section 40 quotes four columns against the one-column rule.

## Tier 1: breaks a section as written

**1. [table: R2 2, R3 1, R3 3] The set-aside at section 27's confidence defangs the sign step.** `reading_confidence` (5962–5965) is `exp(score / n_tests) × (1 − exp(−margin))` with `score = −½ Σ z²`. For a three-arm vertex there is one partition, so `margin` = ∞ and the confidence is the goodness of fit of `murray` and `branch` (plus the flow tests): under calibrated tests (z ~ N(0,1)) the median is 0.64–0.71, 21–25% of readings fall **below 0.5**, and 0–2% exceed 0.99 (100 000 draws, 2–6 tests). The set-aside cost `ln(c / (1 − c))` is then 0.6–0.9 at the median, **negative** below 0.5, against 3.5 for one flip at p = 0.85; `fluxnet.py` line 156 itself says the cost must stay above one flip "or the sign step sacrifices a cheap lumen to every wrong direction (measured: at 0.9 it did)". Measured on binary trees (127 lumens, 70% measured, 15% wrong, top three levels unmeasured, four seeds): default confidences at 0.45 gain +1.6, 0, +4.0, +2.0 points with 0 set aside; with c ~ U(0.5, 0.95) the gains are 0, 0, +1.1, 0 with **9–20 lumens set aside and 0–2 flips**; at 0.15, +4 to +12 becomes +0.7 to +2.7 with 12–28 set aside. With **every direction right** and c ~ U(0.3, 0.95), 15–27 of 127 lumens are set aside at 0.45 (6–14 of 63 on the smaller tree). The plan's sentence "a three-arm vertex has no other reading and is nearly never set aside" is true only of the default 0.999, which the plan does not use. The incoherence is in what the number means: a bifurcation whose daughters miss Murray's law by 1.5σ each is still certainly a junction of three arms (there is no other reading), yet its balance is dropped for free. *Change:* the set-aside cost is the log-odds that the lumen's **structure** is wrong (a three-arm vertex: that it is not a junction; a through join: that the join is wrong, from the join's own evidence), floored above one flip's cost and calibrated on the harness; never the reading's goodness of fit.

**2. [table: R2 2, R3 3, R2 14, R3 9] The plexus tests use an error model and a reading the real burst never has.** `grid_case` draws wrong directions per edge and reads every vertex as one lumen of four; section 32 reads one direction per whole vessel and `geometric_reading` (5898–5899) reads a planar four-way as a crossing. 12×12 grid, measurements per whole vessel (chains of 3–6 edges), 15% of vessels wrong, 70% measured, six seeds: with crossing readings and whole-vessel ids the flips are **3:0, 1:1, 3:2, 2:1, 1:2, 4:0** (vessels right:wrong) and the gain runs −1.7 to +9.8 points at 0.45; with per-edge ids (the test's model) on the same errors the flips are 0:1, 0:1, 0:3, 1:0, 0:0, 0:0 at 0.45 and 0:3, 0:4, 0:4, 3:1, 1:0, 0:2 at 0.15, i.e. mostly right measurements turned. With the one-lumen reading and whole-vessel ids the step is nearly inert at 0.45 (0 to +3 points, 0–1 flips). "In no case does a right measurement cascade" and "flips at least as often right as wrong" hold for the test's model and not for the plan's own description of the errors. *Change:* a plexus test with whole-vessel errors under crossing readings, the three seeds quoted; state that at a crossing plexus the sign step's wrong flips are of the order of its right ones at the notebook's softness.

**3. [table: R1 A4, R2 1, R3 2] The readings–solve loop survives as the rounds.** Section 39 step 6: "once per round, no loop"; step 2 of every round re-reads junctions with the flux test among the tests; rounds run to `ROUNDS_MOST` = 5. The third review's measurement (a two-cycle, flips growing 5 → 22 while the directions worsen) was of exactly this sequence; the cap moved from 3 to 5 and the state at the cap is as arbitrary as before. *Change:* run step 6 in the last round only, or define convergence (no reading changed) and report the non-convergence rate as a column.

**4. [table: R2 2, R3 3] Whole-vessel ids and the lumens come from the same partitions, out of sync.** `join_pieces` (7773) joins through the geometric `partition` and `whole_vessel_flow` (7876) undoes a join whose two sides read against each other; the `undone` set is local and only `len(undone)` is stored (7926). The node keeps its partition, so `lumens_of` still makes a through-lumen there, now with two measurements pointing against each other: impossible, and the network turns one round (3.5 < 4.6, the default two-arm cost), reversing a measurement at a join section 32 had just split because the sides disagreed. Separately, at every through join that survived, both arms carry **one** velocity (`cut["velocity"] = way × reading["velocity"]`, 7953) and `flux(arm)` (5792) is velocity × the piece's mass: the through-lumen's balance is `M_a − M_b` over 0.45 (M_a + M_b), the `density` test of `group_tests` in a looser unit. The flux test cannot contradict a join whose velocity it inherited. *Change:* store the undone joins on the graph and give them c → 0 (or no balance); say in section 40 that a through-lumen of one whole vessel carries no flux information beyond the mass ratio, and that the sign step's unit is the vessel after undoing.

**5. [table: R1 A5, R2 5, R3 4] The bridge prior's unit is the graph's mean flux.** `floor = mean |q̂|` (fluxnet.py 170) and sigma = c / (1 − c) × floor. A graph with one thick vessel (10) and five capillaries (0.1): floor 2.6. A **right** thick bridge at c = 0.5 carries 6.9 of 10 with ±1.6σ imbalances at both its ends, at c = 0.2 carries 1.7 with ±4.3σ, at c = 0.05 carries 0.07 with ±4.5σ: the prior manufactures the mismatch that section 39 reads as flux lost at a free end. A **wrong** capillary bridge (0.1 against 0.01) carries 0.040 at c = 1, 0.039 at c = 0.5, 0.034 at c = 0.2, 0.028 at c = 0.05: doubt changes it by a third, because sigma 0.08–1.4 is no prior on a flux of 0.1. The test passes because its two sides are 4 and 1 on a graph whose mean is 2.5. *Change:* sigma = c / (1 − c) × the free end's own arm flux (|q̂| of the end's arm, or the lumen's mean), not the graph's.

## Tier 2: would make a check mislead

**6. [table: R3 7] The held-out half of the quiet pixels still scores nothing.** `quiet_pixels` (1531) are the background pixels clear of everything that changes; the drawing draws no edge there and only halos (σ_h ≤ 8 px) and new vessels reach them. Splitting them in halves fixes the "1 by construction" of the whitening and leaves the column insensitive to every move on the vessels. *Change:* the whitened RMS over the pixels the drawing touches (edges, cores, halos), against its expectation under the on-edge autocorrelation.

**7. [table: R1 A1, R2 4, R3 5] The core matched filter is the `overlap` test with more pixels, and its reach at a capillary core is unstated.** `partition_reading` already tests the centre's 5×5 max against the sum or max of the arms' densities over `SIGMA_DENSITY` (5845–5848). The plan calls the filter "the one test that can tell a crossing from one lumen of four" without the z it reaches for two capillaries (R ≤ 2, overlap ~9–16 px) under 2 px grain, which depends on the white floor of the noise spectrum that section 38 does not measure. If that z is under 3, "most specific that fits" keeps the crossing at every capillary four-way whatever the composition. *Change:* report the core z by radius on rendered crossings and one-lumen junctions before section 39; say what the filter adds over `overlap`.

**8. [table: R2 13, B8, R3 6] "`extend_ends` only from ends of pieces one field length long" changes nothing.** One field length is `ELONGATION` × half-width = 2.5 × 1.5–2 px = 4–5 px for a capillary (3204, 6846); `centerlines` (2170) already drops every free-ended piece under `SPUR_LENGTH` = 12 px, and `extend_ends` (6790) already skips ends with fewer than four own pixels. The restriction bites only on half-widths above 4.8 px. The third review's spurs are gone because the pruning was kept, not because of this rule. *Change:* drop the clause or state it as what it is.

**9. A missing arm, a wrong speed and a wrong join are one signal, sent to three places.** A through-lumen with trunk 10 in and daughter D out (the sister not in the graph): at 0.45, D = 6 gives −0.7σ, D = 4 −1.5σ, D = 2 −3.4σ with nothing set aside or down-weighted (the sign step's measurement-only sizing makes 8 / (0.45 × 12) = 1.5σ, under the 2.14σ a 0.99 set-aside needs); D = 1 Huber-weights the **trunk's measurement** to 0.61, bends its flux from 10 to 2.7 and reports it at 5.1σ as a wrong speed. At 0.15, D ≤ 4 is set aside and the studentised residuals become exactly 0. Section 39 sends studentised residuals to the speeds, set-asides to the reading's confidence and persistent imbalances to step 3; here they are the same number, and the right speed is the one marked wrong. *Change:* at a two-arm lumen the imbalance goes to step 3 only; the studentised residual is read only where a measurement sits in a lumen with two other measured arms.

**10. With the defaults the set-aside is inert; with section 27's numbers it is hyperactive; nothing lies between.** The balance's residual in its own unit is bounded, |net| / (0.45 × Σ|q|) ≤ 2.22; a 0.999 set-aside needs |z| > 2.63, so a three-arm lumen can never be set aside at 0.45 (an all-in three-arm lumen is flipped, not set aside: checked), and a 0.99 one only when nearly impossible. "A set-aside move ... is rare" holds at 0.45 with defaults (0–4 per solve) and not at 0.15 (18–29 per solve on a crossing plexus with 15% wrong; 5–14 with every direction **right**, from the 15% measurement noise alone). The evidence and the cost are on different scales and the plan calibrates neither. *Change:* calibrate the set-aside cost on the harness as finding 1 says, and quote the set-aside count per softness.

**11. [table: R2 6, R3 13] `SIGMA_FLUX`'s population is capillary bifurcations, applied everywhere.** "Three directions measured" selects junctions whose three arms are trackable, which excludes the thick vessels section 32 flags; the one number then sizes through-lumens (one vessel, where the velocity-profile factor cancels and the physical spread is smaller) and thick lumens alike. The contamination the third review feared is small: with one daughter reversed kept as "possible", the robust sd reads 0.103 for a true 0.10 at p = 0.85 (20 000 draws). The sample size on the real burst is unknown (the notebook prints it, 6178; no outputs are stored). *Change:* one unit per lumen kind (through, bifurcation), the counts printed.

**12. [table: R2 2, R3 8] The sign step's reach is not stated.** Section 40's "Its reach" paragraph is the sizes'. The third review's finding 8 asked for the signs': through-lumens measured on both sides and lumens with every arm measured, inert at a bifurcation off an unmeasured trunk. The table says "the sign step's reach ... stated"; the text does not.

**13. Two-arm joins have no confidence to give.** `read_junction` needs `arms`; a node with two `ends` is joined by `join_pieces` without a reading. `extend_ends` returns a picture (`lines | added`, 6803) and nothing records which forks it made, so "a join made by `extend_ends` carries that extension's confidence" needs provenance code section 37 does not name. *Change:* name it; until then the default 0.99 is the only number, and finding 4's undone joins need another.

**14. A crossing's two lumens each pay the whole reading's confidence.** Setting one aside keeps a reading ("through + nothing") section 27 never scored; setting both aside costs twice what the reading being wrong costs once. *Change:* a vertex's set-aside is per reading, or per lumen at a per-lumen number.

## Tier 3: cost or omission

**15. Cost.** On this machine (4 cores, OpenBLAS), `grid_case`: 1012 edges 9.5 s per solve against the plan's 3 s; 1984 edges **177 s** (flips 24:4) against "20–27 s". The dense `pinv` and the per-move `einsum` over all rows scale worse than the text says; "sparse factorisation beyond a few thousand edges" is needed at two thousand, times rounds, times fixations.

**16. "Parameters are measurements" against "a halo ... fitted thickest first".** The halo is fitted; the price `k_e ln(n_e)` is BIC's, which counts fitted parameters, so a price on measured ones has no derivation and λ absorbs it. A merge changes which windows lie within a core radius, so the measured κ, R jump at the move and ΔJ contains the jump. Say so.

**17. Section 42.** Four columns are quoted for section 40 (directions right, flips, `told_apart`, share failed) under a one-column rule; on the real burst "against `propagate_directions`" is agreement between two inferences.

**18. The λ sweep** has four criteria (repeatability, loose ends, impossible share, cycle share) and no rule for combining them.

## Verified and found correct

- The 30 tests pass (1.4 s). On this machine's trees with default confidences the sign step gains +0 to +4 points at 0.45 and +4 to +12 at 0.15 (four seeds, top three levels unmeasured, 15% wrong), flips 18:5 and 58:2.
- The closed-form flip delta `4 rᵀq − 4 rᵀC_SS r` with `r = P w q̂` matches brute-force re-solves to 1e-4 with a prior on a measured edge and on a free edge (the prior sits in P and hence in C, and contributes 0 to `right`, so `χ²_min = q̂ᵀPq̂ − rightᵀC right` holds), and with a Huber weight of 0.4 on a measurement; in the sign loop every weight is 1 anyway (Huber runs after it).
- The set-aside gain `−r² / (1 − h)` equals the brute-force removal of the weighted row with priors present (four rows, 1e-4). Not resizing `scale` after a set-aside is consistent with the step's measurement-only sizing.
- The move loop lowers the penalised objective at every move (all gains < 0 in twelve traced runs), so a flip and an un-flip cannot alternate; the cap `count + len(lumens) + 1` was never reached.
- `impossible` is computed from the flipped `q̂` (final signs); `flux_at_free_ends` (+1 at end 1) agrees with `lumens_of` (+1 at end 0) and `read_arm` (5407); `directed_cycles` at cutoff 3 drops unmeasured plexus edges (|q| / σ ≈ 0.7) and keeps measured ones (≈ 6.7 at 15% sigma).
- `join_pieces` joins by groups of two of `partition` and by two-end nodes; `whole_vessel_flow` reads sides from windows of one kymograph, undoes disagreeing joins once, and stores the count only; `flux` is `velocity_out × mass` with one velocity per whole vessel.
- `centerlines` prunes every free-ended piece under 12 px in two passes; `extend_ends` skips ends with under four own pixels and extends up to 16 px where the median score is ≥ `T_LOW` / 2; `found_again` and `repeated` give one scalar; `picture_blur` is the `BLUR_SHARE` quantile of the half-widths; `vascnet.synth.render` (synth.py 440–453) takes max within a tree, blurs the transmittance and multiplies across trees.
- Of the handling table, every row's handling is present in the text except the sign step's reach (finding 12) and the loop (finding 3); the `SIGMA_FLUX` unit is the notebook's (`|balance| @ through`).
