# Third adversarial review of `cyclic_graph_recursion_plan.md` (version 3) and `vascnet/fluxnet.py`

Scope: the plan's handling of the first two reviews (its final table), checked against the plan's own text, the prototype and the notebook source (`notebooks/development copy 2.py`; line numbers are from it unless a file is named), plus new problems. Every number below was run (scripts in the session's scratchpad, not committed); the 24 prototype tests pass in 1.1 s. Findings marked **[table]** attack a handling the plan's final table claims.

## Verdict

The prototype's algebra is right and most of the table's handlings are present in the text; what is wrong is what the handlings do when the input is the case the plan was written for. The sign step's pattern rule, added to fix review 2's finding 2, now *manufactures* wrong directions: given a lumen that is wrong (two vessels ending near each other, read as one passing through — the very case `extend_ends` creates by joining free ends within 16 px), it turns a right measurement round at a *correct* neighbouring bifurcation, erases the −3.3σ imbalance that was the only evidence against the wrong lumen, and credits `forced` to the correct bifurcation. The flux test as a section-27 test, added to fix A4, reads every four-arm vertex of a single-depth plexus as a crossing (64 of 64, at `SIGMA_FLUX` 0.45 and 0.15 alike), and the step-6 loop built on it does not converge: it oscillates, and with 15% wrong directions its flips grow from 5 to 22 while the directions get worse. The wrong-speed detector, added to fix finding 7, finds 0 of the ×3 / ÷3 size outliers on a plexus at |z| > 3. The bridges' "softening by 1 / confidence" does not express doubt about an edge: a bridge at confidence 0.01 still conducts the mean of the two sides' fluxes, with the imbalance that would have exposed it divided away, and a rival pair carries the end's flux twice. And the error model the sign step assumes (each measured direction wrong independently) is not the real burst's, where section 32 reads one direction per *whole vessel*: a reversed whole vessel is invisible to the network at 0.45 and 0.3, and at 0.15 the network flips its daughters instead.

Would the plan, as written, improve the automatic annotation of vessels? Section 37 (the graph with confidences) and the measurement-level outputs of section 40 on trees (directions on a 255-edge tree with the thick level unmeasured: +3 to +7 points at 0.45, near-perfect at 0.15; the inferred-direction column) are the parts that would move a number, and the reviews' order of work puts them first; that much is sound. The recursion (38–39) still has no defined template for edges shorter than two core radii, an objective whose "nothing drawn changes outside J" is kept only by letting the reading and the drawing disagree, and a residual column that is 1 by construction. The flux network's feedback into readings (step 6) is, on the evidence here, more likely to hurt a plexus than help it, and the plexus is the case section 35 adds in order to score. In theory the plan improves annotation on tree-like beds with measured arms; on plexuses, at the notebook's softness, it does not yet, and its own checks would not show that, because the softness, the oscillation cap and the specificity rule make the flux test pass wrong readings quietly.

## Tier 1: breaks a section as written

**1. [table: R2 2] The pattern rule, as a hard constraint inside the sign step, turns right measurements round and destroys the evidence against a wrong lumen (`fluxnet.py` 186–221).** On the test graph with every edge measured right, make vertex 3 a wrong through-lumen: e2 (1→3) flows into 3 and dives; e6 is a separate vessel flowing 5→3. The lumen (e2 in, e6 in) is impossible, so a move is forced; the cheapest by the balances is to flip **e2**, the right daughter of the correct bifurcation at vertex 1, not e6. Result: `flipped = [2]`; the vertex-3 lumen's imbalance goes from −3.32σ (`flips=False`) to −0.09σ, its `vertex_tests` z is −0.08 ("fits"), and `forced = [1, 0, 0, 1, 0]` is credited to the bifurcation at vertex 1 as well, because lines 220–221 add `len(best)` to *every* lumen the flipped edge touches. The plan says the forced flips are "evidence against the reading that made the lumen"; here they are evidence against the right reading, and the lumen that was wrong now balances perfectly. Section 27 already rejects an all-in group (`partition_reading` 5842 gives `inf`), so the network's rule only ever acts on lumens made without flow (step 6(a)), and there it acts by corrupting a measurement. *Change:* do not flip under the pattern rule; leave the lumen impossible, output `impossible` per lumen, and let section 27's `in and out` test fail the reading. Attribute `forced` only to lumens that were impossible before the move.

**2. [table: R1 A4, R2 1] The flux z as a section-27 test with "most specific that fits" reads a plexus as crossings, and step 6's loop oscillates.** A 10×10 harmonic grid, every interior vertex one four-arm lumen (the test file's own plexus truth), 70% measured, *all directions right*: after one solve, `vertex_tests` on the three pairings plus one lumen gives at least one pairing with all |z| ≤ `Z_PASS` at **64 of 64** four-arm vertices, at `SIGMA_FLUX` 0.45 and at 0.15. The loop (b)–(d) then changes 12, 7, 4, 4, 4 readings per iteration and recurs (a 2-cycle); with 15% wrong directions, flips rise 5 → 22 → 20 → 20 → 21 → 23 and measured directions right fall 0.768 → 0.736 (seed 1). The plan's stop "until no reading changes or three times" is a cap, not convergence, and the state at the cap is arbitrary. The geometric reading already calls a planar four-way junction a crossing (`geometric_reading`, 5898–5899); the plan's claim that the network's z "can fail a partition where the notebook's test could not" fails none on a plexus. *Change:* the flux z cannot be a pass/fail test at this softness; use it only as a *comparison between equal-count readings* (which the prototype already restricts to) and report the loop's non-convergence rate as a column.

**3. [table: R2 7] The wrong-speed detector does not see wrong sizes on a plexus, and the sign model does not match how directions are read.** 12×12 grid, true directions, 70% measured, 10% of the measured ×3 or ÷3 (σ from the reading itself, 15%): `|studentised| > 3` for **0.00** of the wrong sizes (median |z| 0.97–1.27 against 0.22–0.33 for right ones; 0 of 142 balances down-weighted), three seeds. The toy's 5× trunk at "4σ" (actually 3.65σ) works because a three-arm lumen with tight daughters pins it; a plexus does not. Separately, section 32 reads *one* direction per whole vessel, so a wrong direction reverses a chain of edges, not one. A trunk of six pieces passing five bifurcations, read backwards: at 0.45 and 0.3 the network flips nothing (max |imbalance| 0.45–1.56σ); at 0.15 with 40% daughters it flips the **four daughters** (right → wrong). A subtree of 31 edges reversed in a 127-edge tree: 0 flips at p 0.85 and 0.95. The lumen-wise sets cannot reach a chain, and the prior `2 ln(p/(1−p))` per edge prices a whole-vessel error as k independent errors. *Change:* flip per whole vessel (the unit section 32 reads), seeded from `join_pieces`' paths; state that the studentised residual is a detector only where a lumen's arms are tight.

**4. [table: R1 A5, R2 5] "Softened by 1 / confidence" does not express a doubtful edge, and rivals are not weighed.** Two vessels A (4) and B, bridged; through-lumens at both ends softened by 1/c. B reads 4: the bridge carries 4.00 at c = 1, 0.5, 0.2, 0.1, 0.01 — only its σ grows (2.6 → 255). B reads 1 (the bridge is wrong): the bridge carries 2.42–2.50 at every c, and the imbalance that exposes it falls from −1.80σ (c = 1) to −0.02σ (c = 0.01). Softening removes the test instead of weighing the edge. A rival pair (A's end bridged to B and to C, one through-lumen per bridge): q = [4, 4, 4, 4, 4] — A's flux leaves twice; as a three-arm lumen it splits 3.67 / 3.67 and both bridges live. `lumens_of` makes no balance for a group of one, so "flux lost at every free end" (section 40's output list) is not computed anywhere. *Change:* a bridge is a mixture, not a soft balance: solve with and without it (or with its confidence as a prior on `q = 0`), and report the imbalance in the unsoftened unit.

**5. [table: R1 A1, R2 4] Stopping the windows and the smoothing at the core radius leaves short edges with no fit, and section 38 does not say what happens to them.** A capillary core is 3.5–4 px, a thick one ≥ 8 px (`read_arms`, 5432); with `KNOT_SPACING` 12 an edge under ~2 × core + 12 px has no window, no κ, no R. The renderer draws every lumen "along its splines" and the core's matched filter is "the difference between the two compositions rendered at that core", which needs the arms' κ and R; `read_arm` reads such an edge inside the core (5398), which section 38 cites but then does nothing with. Split crossings (`fuse_split_crossings` fuses pieces under 2.5 widths) and plexus capillary segments are exactly these edges. *Change:* define the fallback (line mass from `read_arm`'s outer half, R from `R_ridge`) and count the share of windowless edges as a column.

## Tier 2: would make a check mislead

**6. [table: R2 13, B8] The "joining steps kept, dropping steps not" pipeline is new code, and `extend_ends` on an unpruned skeleton adds connections.** `centerlines(mask)` (2170) takes no setting; `SPUR_LENGTH` = 0 is a module-global change under which the function is `skeletonize(mask)`. `consolidate_lines` (6806–6823) prunes (`without_short_branches`) *before* and *after* `extend_ends`; the plan's order (crests → close → unpruned skeleton → `extend_ends`) exists nowhere. `extend_ends` skips only ends with fewer than 4 own pixels (6790) and extends every other free end up to 16 px into any line whose median score is ≥ `T_LOW`/2 = 1 noise unit. Proxy (a ridge map of a vascnet render, unpruned skeleton, three seeds): 564–945 spurs per 7–10 k px of skeleton, of which 258–392 are 4–11 px long — each one a degree-3 vertex and an `extend_ends` candidate. Section 37's first-principle check is then scored on a graph with several hundred spurious forks and extensions per frame that section 29's row never had, which the `stub` and `twin` terms must absorb before the comparison means anything. *Change:* keep `SPUR_LENGTH` = 12 for the *skeleton* (a 1–3 px stub is a thinning artefact, not a piece of ridge) and apply "nothing deleted" from `RIDGE_CHILD` upward; or run `extend_ends` only from ends of pieces above `SPLINE_LEAST`.

**7. Section 38's column is 1 by construction.** "The whitened residual's RMS off the graph (1 where the model is right)": the whitening is by the autocorrelation *measured on the quiet pixels off the graph*; its output there has unit variance whatever the model. *Change:* normalise the whitening by the frames' temporal noise or by a held-out set of quiet pixels, and say so.

**8. [table: R2 2] At the notebook's softness the sign step lives on the pattern rule, which never fires where the trunk is unmeasured.** A flipped daughter at a bifurcation, contradicted at that end only: trunk right (10), `SIGMA_FLUX` 0.45 → not flipped (imbalance −2.1σ reported); 0.3 and 0.15 → flipped. Trunk unmeasured → nothing, at every softness (the free arm absorbs any pattern). Trunk read 50 (the size outlier) → not flipped at 0.45 or 0.3 (imbalance −9.5σ / −14σ, the measurement-only sizing has made the balance 27 wide); flipped at 0.15. The table row "one wrong on a leaf, contradicted at one end: turned round" holds through the pattern rule (the test's e7 makes the confluence all-in), i.e. only when every arm is measured — and the plan itself says the thick vessels are unmeasured on the real burst. *Change:* state the sign step's reach: through-lumens measured on both sides and lumens with every arm measured; expect it inert at bifurcations off thick trunks.

**9. [table: R2 14] The plan's grid numbers are from the test's seeds.** "Grids 8×8 to 16×16 … directions right +2 to +4 points, flips at least 7:1 right": seeds 1–3 give +2.6, +2.4, **0.0**, +3.7, +3.1, +3.7, **+1.2**, +2.1, +2.9 points, and flips 2:0, 2:0, 0:0, 8:1, 7:1, **9:2**, **5:1**, 8:1, 11:1. Sensitivity (12×12, four seeds): at p = 0.6 the flips are 59:44 (σ_flux 0.15) to 30:11 (0.7); at p = 0.85 48:29 (0.15) to 16:5 (0.7); the gain in measured directions is +1.4 to +3.6 points everywhere; with 40% measured it is +0.8 and the inferred edges come out about 69% right with median |q|/σ 0.7. The tests assert ≥ 2:1. *Change:* quote ranges over seeds and the test's own threshold.

**10. The objective J is not what the moves are judged by, and the reading and the drawing can disagree.** `n_e` is defined as "its pixels over the correlation area of the noise there": the single divisor the whitening was adopted to replace, now in the price (a 4× error costs 1.4 per parameter; minor, but the text contradicts itself). The per-window R, κ fits (`profile_across`, raw) and the halo fit are least squares on the raw profile, not moves under `χ²_w`. One whitening filter per canvas cannot be right for a canvas that holds both off-graph noise (2 px grain) and along-edge misfit (12–36 px), and the plan measures two autocorrelations without saying which whitens what. Step 2 and 3b: the reading decides lumens and merges, "J can veto but not propose" — after a veto the flux network's lumens follow the reading while the rendering keeps the old composition; the next round's core filter is read on that rendering. The termination argument covers J, not this pair. *Change:* define `n_e` from the whitened template's norm; name the whitening per region; state what happens after a veto (the reading reverts, or the drawing follows without a price).

**11. Integration details that would silently change results.** A NaN z passed into `partition_reading` fails the partition (`(abs(values) <= Z_PASS).all()` is False on NaN, 5850); `group_tests` only adds `flux` when every arm has one (5819–5820), so the NaN rule of `vertex_tests` must be mapped to "no test" explicitly. `directed_cycles`' cutoff is unspecified; on 12×12 / 16×16 grids after the network the count is 2–9 at cutoff 0 (unmeasured edges at |q|/σ ≈ 0.7 take part), 1–2 at cutoff 3 (truth 0; as read 1–2). A reversed half of a loop is a directed cycle with zero imbalance at every through-lumen; only `directed_cycles` sees it, and the plan sends cycles nowhere. The balance-row imbalance is exact only at weight 1: with a Huber-down-weighted row (w = 0.32, σ_flux 0.1) the code gives −9.75 against −9.26 by brute-force leave-one-out (the identity is for the weighted row; the plan's definition is the unweighted one).

## Tier 3: cost or omission

**12. [table: R2 15] Cost.** Dense `pinv`, this machine (4 cores, OpenBLAS): 480 edges 0.8 s; 1012 edges 3.0–3.6 s; 1984 edges 20–27 s per `solve_flux_network` (with flips). "A few seconds at a thousand edges" holds; at two thousand it is half a minute, times three reading iterations times five rounds per fixation. `_flip_sets` above `most` = 6 proposes singles and complements only: a pair of wrong arms in a seven-arm lumen is never a move.

**13. `SIGMA_FLUX` re-measured under the readings it will judge.** Section 40 re-measures the spread "on the whole-vessel graphs" under section 27's geometric readings; a wrong reading widens the spread, which softens the test that should fail it. Measure it on three-arm junctions whose three directions are measured and possible, or on the synthetic bursts, and say which.

**14. Section 36's column needs harness code that does not exist.** `junction_metrics` scores type and position through `cluster_truth`; a fused "complex" truth has no `n`. The 4 px ring itself is fine: on a rendered capillary bifurcation (R 1.5, blur 0.8, transmittance blur) the density ring at 4 px shows three peaks for daughters 40°, 60° and 90° apart, and a 45° crossing gives four; this is the density, not the oriented score, which section 36's check 1 still has to show.

**15. Small misstatements.** The five-times trunk is at 3.65σ, not 4σ. C3 (the flow field) is still open. `compare_readings` drops the log-determinant terms, as review 2 said; the plan restricts it to equal counts, which is fine, but calls it "the flux's evidence".

## Verified and found correct

- `chi2_min = q̂ᵀPq̂ − rightᵀC·right`, and the change under a set S, `4 rᵀq − 4 rᵀC_SS r`, match brute-force re-solves to 1e-6 on single, pair and cross-lumen sets (line 205).
- The Woodbury update in `vertex_tests` (`C + CXᵀ(I − XCXᵀ)⁻¹XC`, `q + gain·Xq`) is the exact removal of the vertex's rows; the candidate scores do not depend on the enforced reading, also with a 5× trunk (differences ≤ 0.01) and with a flipped daughter (the flip is made under every enforced reading); a right bifurcation and confluence are not failed by a misread crossing next to them (|z| ≤ 0.34 at σ_flux 0.15).
- `lumen_scale` uses `|balance| @ through`, the sum of |q| over *all* the lumen's arms: the notebook's unit (5820), as the table claims for R2 6.
- The measurement studentised residual is exact under weights (the weighted-row identity); the balance identity is exact at weight 1.
- `directed_cycles` is Tarjan plus one BFS per component; the 6×6 both-ways grid gives one cycle; the confidence cutoff excludes edges below it.
- `vascnet.synth.render` (synth.py 448–452) takes max within a tree, blurs the *transmittance*, multiplies across trees; the notebook's generator blurs the density per tree and sums (6416–6418); it runs no background fit and returns `valid` all true (6430); clusters move at one speed per segment (plug flow, 6386).
- `static_scores` saved by section 29 is `orientation_scores` (7178–7181), the pre-interaction score; `repeated` returns one scalar per fixation (4235); `fuse_split_crossings` runs inside `vessel_graph` (5545); `contract_cores` already drops spurs shorter than the core from the nodes' ends (5364, 5380), so "nothing is deleted" is already not literally true of the existing graph code.
- `join_pieces`, section 32's one kymograph per whole vessel, and `direction_counts`/`reliability` are as the plan cites them.
- On binary trees with the top three levels unmeasured and 15% wrong, the sign step gains +3 to +7 points at 0.45 with 52 right / 3 wrong flips over four seeds, and reaches 0.98–1.00 at 0.15.
