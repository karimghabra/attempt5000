## What it means

- **Thick cores (R ≥ 3)** are told apart at every blur the generator makes (`told_apart` 1.00 in all 12 cells). The filter can decide composition there.
- **Capillaries** are told apart in focus. At 1.5 px, R = 1.5 gives z 5.0 (`told_apart` 1.00) and R = 1 gives 2.7 (0.97). By 3.5 px they are lost (0.62 and 0.72), as the plan says.
- **Under one lumen, z is not always centred on 0.** It goes down to −3.1 (R 2, 1.5 px), −3.9 (R 3, 2 px) and −8.2 (R 5, 3.5 px). The arms' κ and R, fitted outside the core, misjudge what one lumen draws there, and the error grows with the blur.
  - `told_apart` is unaffected, because it compares the two distributions directly.
  - A fixed cut at `Z_PASS` on z alone would call some one-lumen cores "two vessels less than expected". Step 7's move therefore asks for both: z below `Z_PASS` and z at least `Z_PASS` below the expected z.
