## What was built

Three new notebook cells after section 34:

- `plan-35`: a markdown cell describing the section.
- `generator-functions`: a definition cell, added to `DEFINITION_CELLS` so that a fresh kernel loads it.
- `section-35`: the cell that runs the checks and the counts.

`synthetic_burst_35(seed, kind, **things)` switches each of the plan's twelve things on by name. The thirteenth, "confluences, both flow directions equally, as now", is unchanged from section 28. With no thing switched on, it draws the same random numbers in the same order as `synthetic_burst(seed)` and does the same arithmetic, so its burst is the same to the bit. What a thing needs on top is drawn from a stream of its own. Section 28's code is untouched.

There are 20 tests in `tests/test_section35.py`, and the suite now has 76 tests, all passing. The tests check:

- the old seeds, against digests of the untouched code;
- flux conserved at every node;
- loops counted by the graph's cycle rank;
- gaps fading the chord to `SYNTH_GAP_DEPTH`;
- a vessel of radius 1 keeping its mass within 3% anywhere on the pixel grid;
- both plexus kinds made from the same lines;
- the second fixation being the first moved by its map;
- `n` and `lumens` on every junction, and `junction_arms` of a complex junction;
- the velocity profile's `v_max`.

## Choices the plan left open, and what was chosen

The plan names these constants but not their values (except `SYNTH_BLUNT` ≈ 3). None of them affects an old seed.

| constant | value | why |
|---|---|---|
| `SYNTH_LOOP`, `SYNTH_GAP`, `SYNTH_END` | 0.5, 0.25, 0.25 | a few of each per burst (table above) |
| `SYNTH_GAP_LENGTH`, `SYNTH_GAP_DEPTH` | 24 px, 0.15 | a gap shorter than `BRIDGE_REACH` (≥ 16 px) for every vessel, deep enough to break the ridge |
| capillary trunk radius; speeds | 1.5–2 px (daughters 0.9–1.7); 0.8–2.5 px/frame | "R of 1 to 2 px" |
| `SYNTH_FILE` | 2 px | at or below this radius the cells go in single file (plug flow); above it the profile has `SYNTH_BLUNT` = 3 |
| halo | ± 0.5 × the tree's density blurred over 3 radii, on trees with a vessel of 3 px or more | about a tenth of the central density, either sign |
| plexus | 3 + 4 lines, 40% of them thick (3–4.5 px); anastomotic fluxes from a resistor network | the same lines are read both ways |
| second fixation | shift of 8–30 px per axis, either sign, sub-pixel; same tissue texture moved; fresh clusters and noise | "the same truth imaged again with fresh noise and a known shift" |
| `background` | section 12's `fit_background` on the mean recorded frame | the plan's first option, so the harness is not marked approximate |
| `SYNTH_FINE` | 7 × 7 points per pixel | 3 × 3 loses up to 7% of a radius-1 vessel's mass; 7 keeps it within 2% (measured) |
