## Why the window is off

The plan set the 110 px margin on renders of noise-level strength. The real render answers at 13 to 65 noise units in the window, and the error grows with the answer. These figures are from the margin script, which uses the real frame's constants on the 8-bit copy of the render that the cell saves:

| width (px) | largest answer in the window | off by, margin 110 px | 160 px | 220 px | 280 px |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.5 | 55.1 | 0.026 | 0.020 | 0.013 | 0.022 |
| 2.1 | 59.1 | 0.013 | 0.010 | 0.006 | 0.010 |
| 3 | 64.9 | 0.005 | 0.004 | 0.002 | 0.004 |
| 4.2 to 12 | 19 to 49 | ≤ 0.003 | ≤ 0.003 | ≤ 0.003 | ≤ 0.002 |
| 17 | 12.8 | **0.041** | 0.004 | 0.002 | 0.002 |
| the window's time, all widths | | 1.1 s | 1.2 s | 1.8 s | 2.0 s |

- **The widest channel** (width 17, a 42 px field) is cut short at 110 px. At 160 px it is within 0.005.
- **The narrow channels** keep a floor of 0.01–0.03 at every margin. This is float32 rounding at answers of 55–65.

So 0.005 is reachable only where the render is about as strong as the noise. A larger margin fixes the truncation and costs time. The plan's text now says so. `WINDOW_MARGIN` stays at 110 px: a gate is not passed by retuning after the run.
