A join is right when, within 10 px of it, every piece lies on one true segment, or on two segments that meet in a node of one lumen. A spot check of the 19 false joins of lines in six held-out bursts found:

- 16 fuse a vessel's line onto a ridge line of 12 px or more that lies on no vessel (3 of them at the frame's edge);

So the closing mostly attaches noise lines to vessels. This bears on step 3: a closing-made join must not get structural confidence 1 − ε.

## Where the plan's text and the measurements disagreed (the plan is fixed in this commit)

1. **`RIDGE_PRECISION`.** The plan said "about 0.5 px on the staircase, half that with the offsets". Measured on 120 pieces of eight bursts, against the true polyline and away from junctions, it is 0.46 px at the pixels' centres and **0.38 px** with the offsets. At 0.25 px the knot refinement takes 6 px knots on 8% of the generator's smooth pieces, and a split pays on 30%.
2. **The points are not independent.** Their deviations are correlated over 1.9 points (sum of ρ² over lags, median). The BIC now counts samples as points / `RIDGE_CORRELATION`, as the plan already does for its topology residual.
3. **The split.** In the plan's rule a piece is split when halving would take it below 6 px. That cannot produce what its own test asks for: a halving over the whole piece never pays for a local corner (a 90° corner on a 79-point piece gains less than its price at every precision tried). Priced by residual alone, a split also cuts ridges pulled 1–1.8 px aside near crossings as readily as a corner. The split is now its own priced move at the point of largest residual, taken only at a bend of more than `FUSE_STRAIGHT`.
4. **"On the generator's smooth vessels the refinement fires on nothing."** Over 16 bursts, 24 of 25 pieces on one segment away from junctions keep 12 px knots and one is split. Of 201 pieces that pass a junction, 10 are split. The test now asks for at least 90% over eight bursts.
5. **The sub-pixel places.** The plan names section 21's crest neurons. Section 29's saved inputs have no interaction field, so the crests are taken from the pre-interaction score, the score the ridges come from. On bursts 1 and 2 this gives a crest on 84–95% of the points against 78–89%, and a residual of 0.22 / 0.14 px against 0.25 / 0.16. Initialisation step 1 now says so.
