## What it means

- **Check 1.** Densities add across lumens, so a thin vessel crossing a thick one stays visible over it, and its own-ring arm is strong while the blur is no more than the real burst's. The second reading at the thin width is built (`width_channels`), but it never sees what the winner misses. A thin vessel blurred by 3 px answers at a wider width, which the winner already reads. It is therefore not used.
- **Check 2.** The plan's rule, which keeps the ring with the largest rate, loses a thin daughter off a thick trunk. The daughter starts inside the trunk's lumen and appears only on the rings outside it. There the geometric mean with its weaker arm (about 45) stays below the two thick arms' rate on a smaller ring (about 98). The ring with the most arms among those above `T_HIGH` (`most_arms_ring`) reads it as three arms at 0.8 and 1.5 px. The plan's rule was kept, and both are in the plan's text and known limits.
- **Check 3.** A crossing split into two forks 8–11 px apart is read as four arms at the midpoint. This is the case `fuse_split_crossings` handles for straight arms; the cell sees it whatever the arms do.
