## What I think it means

1. **The balance's softness caps every inferred direction.** A lumen's balance may miss by `SIGMA_FLUX` × the flux through it. An edge whose direction comes only through one junction (a trunk from two measured daughters) therefore has σ ≥ `SIGMA_FLUX` × 2|q|, and its |q| / σ can never exceed about 1 / (2 `SIGMA_FLUX`).

   On this generator, with the velocity profile in, `SIGMA_FLUX` is measured at 0.30 for bifurcations and 0.38 for through-lumens. The cap is therefore about 1.7σ. The network can only reach 2σ where several balances agree, which is rare on trees. So it can at best match the sign rule of `propagate_directions` at that rule's precision, and it does not improve on it.

2. **The sign step has little to correct, and on section 30's bursts it corrects wrongly.**
   - The pipeline's measured directions are 92–94% right, so wrong directions are rare.
   - On section 35's trees the sign step flipped one direction right and one wrong.
   - On section 30's bursts it turned 13 right measurements round and corrected none. Their softness is tight there (through 0.18, bifurcation 0.24), and p is 0.82 (flip cost 3.0).

   The plan's measured gain (+3 to +7 points on binary trees with the thick level unmeasured) came from idealized graphs. Here the graphs are the pipeline's own, and most of their edges are measured.

3. **The plexus is inert, as the plan expects:** no direction inferred at 2σ and no flip on either kind.

4. **What would change the outcome is a choice for the plan's author, not for this branch.** The gate was not changed. Some options:
   - a softness that does not scale with the flux through the junction;
   - a cutoff for inferred directions that is not a fixed |q| / σ;
   - comparing all directions after the network, not only inferred ones, with the measured ones included.

## Choices made, and where the plan was changed

- **λ (the plan is fixed in this commit).** The plan said λ is chosen at step 3 from this run's shares. λ prices structural moves only, and none exists before the recursion, so every λ gives the same model. Step 3 measures the reference shares, and step 7 sweeps λ against them.
- **The rival "through pair + free end".** It is not added to section 27's partitions, because that would change section 27's readings, which section 34's numbers depend on. Instead, a three-arm bifurcation whose baseline saw the thin stem beyond the far wall (`bifurcation, T`) gets the share right of such readings as its structural confidence. On trees that was 0 of 8.
- **p.** The three-arm p is used only with at least `FLUX_LEAST` = 10 such junctions, else the sides' p. Section 34's rule (the three-arm p whenever it is defined) gives 0.5 on the plexus, from one or two junctions, at which a flip costs nothing.
- **The defaults of Part III.** Default 1 (the kymograph's line mass M for the flux) mattered: every measured flux is M × velocity. Its cross-check against π κ R² waits for κ (step 5). Defaults 2, 3 and 4 were not used.
- **The unit tests.** `join_provenance`'s test holds: the undone joins equal section 32's count. The rebuilt consolidation equals `consolidate_lines` to the pixel. Eleven tests are in `tests/test_section40.py`; the suite has 98, all passing.
